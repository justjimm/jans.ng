Olujimi Adewole founded the firm in 2014 as Jimi Adewole Negotiators | Solicitors and is its Immediate Past Principal Partner. He leads the firm's energy, banking and fintech, and real estate work, and its arbitration, mediation and negotiation practice.

His energy work covers oil, gas, power and renewables: licensing, regulatory compliance, project development, financing and commercial contracts. It has included technical partnership agreements between energy service companies, LNG infrastructure financing and capital raising for energy projects. In negotiation and disputes, he has negotiated cross-border commercial and financial services agreements and recovered debts for corporate clients, including from public bodies. His property work covers transactions, title due diligence, land use compliance and property disputes.

Olujimi holds an LL.B from the University of Lagos. He trained at the Nigerian Law School, Abuja, and was called to the Nigerian Bar in 2012. His early practice was at Aelex and F.O. Akinrele & Co., and he has since worked in both law firm and in-house roles.
