Simisola Idowu is the Principal Partner and Head of Practice at JANS. She advises and represents clients in employment and labour law, insurance law and general civil litigation, and leads the firm's capital markets work.

Her employment work covers contracts, workplace policies, discipline, termination, redundancy and employment disputes, for employers and employees. In insurance, she acts for insurers, insured parties and brokers on claims, policy interpretation, regulatory compliance and disputes. Her litigation practice includes personal injury, professional negligence and other civil claims.

Simisola holds a degree in Economics from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, and was called to the Nigerian Bar after training at the Nigerian Law School. She has practised law for over a decade.
